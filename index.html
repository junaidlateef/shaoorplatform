About SHAOOR

Art. Heritage. Youth.

SHAOOR is a Pakistan-wide creative arts platform built by young people from different cities — united by one belief: every creator deserves a stage.

We provide structured training, exhibitions, mentorship, and a verified member community — across 7 creative societies and 6 provincial chapters.

SHAOOR also honours Pakistan's great artists of the past, bringing their legacy to the new generation before it is lost to time.

TrainingRegular workshops and mentorship across all 7 societies.
ChaptersPunjab, KPK, Sindh, Balochistan, Kashmir & Gilgit-Baltistan.

The Founder's Vision

The Woman Behind SHAOOR

"SHAOOR is not just a platform — it's a movement to reclaim our cultural identity through art. Every artist has a story, and this platform is for them to tell it."

Fatima tu Zahra founded SHAOOR in Sahiwal with a belief that creativity is the most powerful tool a community has. A Chemistry graduate and passionate creative practitioner, she built SHAOOR to be a space where artists teach, learners grow, and Pakistan's cultural heritage is preserved for future generations.
